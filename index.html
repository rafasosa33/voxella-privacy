Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VOXELLA ("VOXELLA", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the VOXELLA Android application (the "App"). VOXELLA is a voice-to-text application that lets you convert speech into text. We are committed to respecting your privacy and explaining what information is handled by the App and by third-party advertising services.

1. Information You Provide

VOXELLA does not require you to create an account or register to use its main features. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, telephone number, or password to use the App.

When you use voice-to-text features, you create spoken input and transcription text. Transcriptions that you choose to save are stored locally on your device, subject to the App's available features and your device settings.

2. Microphone and Voice Processing

VOXELLA requests microphone access so that you can speak and convert your speech into text. The microphone is used when you actively start a voice-recognition or dictation feature.

VOXELLA is designed to process voice input locally on your Android device. The App does not upload your voice recordings to VOXELLA-owned servers for transcription. Audio may be handled temporarily in device memory while speech recognition is taking place. VOXELLA does not intentionally retain raw voice recordings after the transcription operation, unless a specific feature clearly tells you otherwise.

Important: Voice recognition can depend on the speech recognition engine, language pack, and Android services available on your device. If your device's selected speech recognition service processes audio through its own systems, that service's provider may handle audio under its own privacy policy. Check the speech recognition settings and provider information on your device. Do not use an online recognition service if you require fully offline processing.

3. Transcription Text and Local Storage

Text generated by the App may be displayed and, where the App provides a save feature, stored locally on your device. VOXELLA does not operate its own cloud account or server to store, synchronize, or back up your transcriptions.

You are responsible for any action you take to copy, share, export, or otherwise send transcription text outside the App. When you share text using another application, that application's privacy policy applies to its subsequent handling of the information.

4. Information We Collect

We do not use a separate analytics service to track how you use VOXELLA. We do not directly collect account details or precise location through the App's voice-to-text features.

The App displays third-party advertisements. The advertising provider's software may collect or receive certain technical information as described in Section 6. This advertising-related processing is separate from the local handling of your voice input and transcription text by VOXELLA.

5. Permissions

The App may request the following Android permission, depending on the features available:

  • Microphone: to capture speech when you actively use voice-to-text.

You can manage or revoke app permissions in your Android device settings. If you revoke microphone permission, voice input features may no longer work, although other available features may remain usable.

6. Advertising and Third-Party Services

VOXELLA displays advertisements provided by a third-party advertising service. Depending on the provider, its configuration, your region, and your device settings, the advertising SDK may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers or other device identifiers;
  • IP address and general device or network information;
  • Ad impressions, clicks, and other advertising interaction events;
  • Approximate location inferred from an IP address, where used by the provider;
  • Diagnostic, security, and fraud-prevention information.

The advertising provider may use this information to deliver, limit, personalize, measure, and improve advertisements and to detect invalid activity or fraud, subject to its own privacy policy and applicable law. VOXELLA does not use a separate analytics service, but an advertising provider may process ad-related events for advertising measurement and reporting.

The specific advertising provider must be identified in this policy once the advertising SDK used in the released App is confirmed. Add the provider's name and a link to its privacy policy before publication if applicable.

7. How Information Is Shared

VOXELLA does not sell your voice recordings or transcription text. The App does not send voice recordings or saved transcriptions to VOXELLA-owned servers for storage or transcription.

Advertising-related information may be transmitted directly by the advertising SDK to the advertising provider and its partners as needed to provide advertising services. Those parties handle information under their own privacy policies and terms.

If you choose to share or export a transcription using another app or service, you are choosing to send that information to the recipient or service you select.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

VOXELLA does not maintain server-side copies of your voice recordings or saved transcriptions. Transcription text saved by the App remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App, subject to Android backup and device settings.

Advertising-related information is retained by the advertising provider according to that provider's retention practices and privacy policy.

9. Data Security

We aim to limit the handling of voice input by processing it locally in the App's intended workflow and not uploading it to VOXELLA-owned servers. The security of information stored on your device also depends on your device, operating system, and security settings. We recommend keeping Android and VOXELLA up to date and using an appropriate device screen lock. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

VOXELLA is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require account registration. Advertising must be configured and used in accordance with applicable laws and the advertising provider's requirements for the audience and age group targeted by the App.

11. Your Choices and Rights

You can manage your information and privacy by:

  • Granting or revoking microphone permission in Android settings;
  • Deleting transcription text saved within the App, where that feature is available;
  • Clearing VOXELLA's app storage or uninstalling the App to remove locally stored app data;
  • Reviewing advertising and privacy controls available on your Android device and Google account, where applicable;
  • Contacting us with questions or privacy requests.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, its features, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
